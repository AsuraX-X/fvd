2. SCOPE

This policy explains what personal data we collect when you visit or use the
FVDlance website at [DOMAIN] (the "Platform"), why we collect it, who we share
it with, how long we keep it and what rights you have.


3. THE DATA WE COLLECT

3.1 Account information
When you create an account we collect your name, email address and password.
Passwords are stored in hashed form and we cannot read them. If you sign in
with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from
Google; we do not receive your Google password.

We also record whether your email address has been verified.

3.2 Profile information
You may add information to your profile, including your first name and
surname, avatar image, location, headline, bio, website, external links, and
(for experts) specialty, rate and selected portfolio projects (images, titles
and links).

3.3 Expert applications
If you apply to become an expert, we collect the information in your
application: name, email address, specialty, bio, portfolio link and/or
portfolio file, and any other links you provide.

3.4 Enquiries
If you send us an enquiry, we collect your name, email address and the brief
or message you send.

3.5 Messages and attachments
When you use the messaging feature, we store the content of your messages, any
files you attach (with their file names and sizes), when each message was
sent, and when it was read.

3.6 Reviews and saved experts
If you leave a review of an expert, we store your rating, comment and the date.
If you save an expert to your list, we record that.

3.7 Technical and session data
When you sign in we create a session, and our authentication system records
information such as your IP address and browser/device type (user agent) for
security purposes, for example to detect suspicious sign-ins and prevent abuse.
[CONFIRM WITH BETTER-AUTH / DASH PLUGIN EXACTLY WHAT IS LOGGED.]

Our hosting provider may also process standard server log data (such as IP
address, request time and pages requested) to operate and secure the service.


4. HOW AND WHY WE USE YOUR DATA

We use your personal data to:

a) Create and manage your account and let you sign in
   (legal basis: performance of a contract with you).
b) Verify your email address and send you service emails such as verification
   links (contract).
c) Display expert profiles, portfolio work and reviews publicly on the
   Platform (contract; for experts, also your consent to public display given
   when you apply).
d) Review expert applications and decide whether to approve them
   (contract / legitimate interests in maintaining the quality of the
   Platform).
e) Deliver messages between users, including in real time (contract).
f) Respond to enquiries you send us (legitimate interests / steps taken at
   your request before entering into a contract).
g) Keep the Platform secure, prevent fraud and abuse, and enforce our Terms of
   Service (legitimate interests).
h) Comply with legal obligations (legal obligation).

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your data for advertising
and we do not use automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly
significant effects on you.

We only use cookies that are strictly necessary for the Platform to work,

If we introduce analytics or other non-essential cookies in the future, we will

9. HOW LONG WE KEEP YOUR DATA

- Account and profile data: for as long as your account exists.
- Messages and attachments: for as long as both participants' accounts exist.
  When either participant deletes their account, the whole conversation
  (including the other person's messages and attachments) is deleted.
- Uploaded files (avatars, portfolio images, application portfolio files and
  message attachments): permanently deleted from our file storage when you
  delete your account.
- Expert applications: for as long as your account exists [OR: X months after
  a decision]. Applications submitted without an account are kept for
  [X MONTHS] unless you ask us to delete them.
- Enquiries: [X MONTHS/YEARS]. Enquiries are kept after account deletion so we
  have a record of the correspondence, but are no longer linked to your
  account.
- Reviews: kept after account deletion as "Former member" (see section 5).
- Sessions: until they expire or you sign out.
- Server/security logs: [X DAYS], as set by our providers.

We may keep data longer where needed to comply with legal obligations or to
resolve disputes.
